#MediAlert
MediAlert is an AI-powered medicine reminder web application designed to help users take their medications on time and stay consistent with their treatment plans.
The application allows users to schedule reminders for their medicines, receive timely notifications, and access simple AI-generated information about the medicine's common uses and general precautions. MediAlert aims to reduce missed doses, improve medication adherence, and make healthcare management easier for everyone, especially elderly users and people with busy lifestyles.
Key Features
💊 Add and manage medicine reminders
⏰ Timely medication notifications
🤖 AI-powered explanation of medicines
📋 Simple and user-friendly interface
🔒 Privacy-focused design
Our Mission
Our mission is to use AI to make medication management simple, accessible, and reliable, helping users maintain healthier lives by never missing an important dose.
